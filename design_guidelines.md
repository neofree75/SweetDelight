# Design Guidelines for Modern Pastry Shop Website

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from premium bakery and artisanal food websites like King Arthur Baking, Magnolia Bakery, and Ladurée, emphasizing warmth, craftsmanship, and indulgence while maintaining modern e-commerce functionality.

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Cream base: 45 15% 95% (warm off-white backgrounds)
- Soft pink: 350 25% 85% (accent elements, buttons)
- Light brown: 25 20% 75% (text, borders, secondary elements)

**Supporting Colors:**
- Deep brown: 25 30% 25% (primary text, headers)
- Muted rose: 345 20% 70% (hover states, highlights)
- Pure white: 0 0% 100% (cards, modals)

**Dark Mode:**
- Primary: 25 15% 15% (dark brown base)
- Secondary: 350 10% 25% (muted pink-brown)
- Accent: 45 20% 85% (warm cream for contrast)

### B. Typography
**Primary Font:** Playfair Display (elegant serif for headers)
**Secondary Font:** Inter (clean sans-serif for body text)
**Accent Font:** Dancing Script (handwritten style for special elements)

**Hierarchy:**
- Hero titles: Playfair Display, 48px bold
- Section headers: Playfair Display, 32px semibold
- Product names: Inter, 20px semibold
- Body text: Inter, 16px regular
- Accent text: Dancing Script, 18px regular

### C. Layout System
**Spacing:** Tailwind units of 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16
- Micro spacing: p-2, m-2 (8px)
- Standard spacing: p-4, m-4 (16px)
- Section spacing: p-8, m-8 (32px)
- Large spacing: p-12, m-12 (48px)

### D. Component Library

**Navigation:**
- Clean header with pastry shop logo
- Soft pink primary buttons with rounded corners
- Dropdown menus with cream backgrounds

**Product Cards:**
- White cards with subtle shadows
- Rounded corners (rounded-lg)
- Soft pink "Add to Cart" buttons
- Light brown price text

**Forms:**
- Cream-colored input backgrounds
- Light brown borders
- Soft pink focus states
- Rounded input fields

**Shopping Cart:**
- Overlay design with blurred background
- White card container
- Clear quantity controls
- Prominent checkout button in soft pink

## Visual Treatment

### Hero Section
- Large hero image featuring artisanal pastries
- Overlay with soft gradient (cream to transparent)
- Centered logo and tagline in Playfair Display
- "Order Now" button with variant="outline" and blurred background

### Product Showcase
- Grid layout with high-quality pastry photography
- Warm lighting emphasizing golden-brown textures
- Clean product cards with generous whitespace

### Trust Elements
- Customer testimonials in elegant card format
- Awards/certifications with subtle styling
- Opening hours and location in readable format

## Images
**Hero Image:** Large, professional photo of a bakery display case filled with colorful pastries, warm lighting, shallow depth of field focusing on featured items

**Product Images:** High-resolution photos of individual pastries on clean white/cream backgrounds, consistent lighting and styling

**About Section:** Cozy bakery interior showing ovens, baking process, or pastry chef at work

**Background Elements:** Subtle flour dusting textures, watercolor-style pastry illustrations as decorative elements

## Key Design Principles
- **Warmth & Comfort:** Evoke feelings of home baking and artisanal craftsmanship
- **Premium Quality:** Clean layouts that showcase product photography
- **Trust & Reliability:** Clear information hierarchy and professional presentation
- **Appetite Appeal:** Color choices and imagery that enhance food photography
- **Accessibility:** Strong contrast ratios and readable typography across all devices
